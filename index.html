About

— Zhenai Xiao · 肖珍爱

I help brands speak the language of the markets they're entering — credibly, consistently, and strategically.

I'm a Communications & Market Development Consultant based in Montréal, Canada. My career has been shaped inside the institutions that define how Western countries communicate with the world — federal government, public diplomacy, international advocacy.

That experience is the foundation of how I work with brands today. I've written for cabinet ministers, advised foreign governments, and worked across four languages and three continents. The same precision and cultural fluency that institutional communications demand are exactly what's required when a brand wants to be taken seriously in a new market.

I work primarily with Chinese brands expanding into North America — where strong products alone no longer earn trust. Western buyers evaluate brand presence, consistency, and cultural fluency before they ever evaluate the product itself. My role is to close that gap.
